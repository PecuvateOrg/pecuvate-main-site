title: Preparing a document for signing

---

PecuSign does not write or check what your document says. That stays with you and your AI app.
This guide covers what PecuSign needs so that signing goes smoothly and the signed copy holds up.

## The document

- **Send the final version as a PDF.** Signers sign exactly the file you send, and its fingerprint is
  recorded, so any later change means sending it again. Remove "DRAFT" marks, comments and tracked
  changes first.
- **Leave out signature lines and blank boxes.** PecuSign adds a signatures page and a certificate to
  the end of the document. Instead, a short line works well where the signatures would go, for
  example: *"This document is signed electronically. The signatures and the record of signing are
  attached at the end."*
- **Name the signers in the document the same way you will list them in PecuSign**, with the capacity
  they sign in, for example "Jane Smith, Director, for and on behalf of Example Ltd".
- **The date:** if the document takes effect when it is signed, say that it is dated on the date of
  the last signature. PecuSign records the exact time of each signature.
- Keep it under 20 MB.

## The signers

For each signer, PecuSign needs:
- **their full name**, as it appears in the document;
- **their own email address.** It must be a mailbox that only they read. The code sent there is what
  proves who signed, so shared addresses (info@, office@, a team inbox) must not be used, and two
  signers must never share one;
- **their role**, if you want it shown (for example "Director" or "Witness");
- **their step**, if the order matters. Everyone in step 1 signs first, in any order. Step 2 is emailed
  when step 1 is complete, and so on. If the order does not matter, put everyone in step 1.

## Before you send

1. Check the PDF is the final one.
2. Check every name and email address, and that no two signers share a mailbox.
3. Check the steps.
4. Send. Your AI app shows you who will be emailed and sends only when you confirm.

## For AI apps

When a person asks you to send a document through PecuSign:
1. Write or finalise the document with them, following the points above, and produce a PDF.
2. Ask for every signer's name, email address, role and step. Do not guess an email address. If two
   signers have the same address, or an address looks shared, ask before going on.
3. Create the draft, then show the person the preview of who will be emailed and ask them to confirm
   before you send.
4. When it is complete, remind them that PecuSign does not keep a copy unless they have paid storage:
   they should download the signed PDF and the `.tsr` timestamp file and save them somewhere safe
   within 30 days.

PecuSign does not give legal advice. Whether a document needs witnesses, a deed, or another formality
is for the person and their advisers to decide.
