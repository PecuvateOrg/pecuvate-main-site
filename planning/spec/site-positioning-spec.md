## Why this spec exists

The 2026-07-21 copy pass correctly removed the stale "venture studio" framing but replaced it with the **internal** identity ("the operating system of BFG Alliance") — org-chart language that fails the stranger test and reads as "not for hire." The KB holds two layers of truth and never ruled which one is public-facing. This spec makes that ruling.

## The two voice layers (the core ruling)

| Layer | Statement | Where it's used |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal identity** | Pecuvate is the operating system of BFG Alliance — the system that identifies, tests, builds, and scales ventures. | KB, internal strategy, group documents. On the website: only as depth (Ecosystem section), never the headline. |
| **External positioning** | Pecuvate combines innovative thinking with technological application to solve structural problems — it architects business models and **builds** the systems that run them. | The website, proposals, public materials. This leads. |

The external layer is not new invention — it is assembled from what the KB already says:

- Group mission (`entities/bfg-alliance.md`): *"…through innovative thinking and the strategic application of technology."*
- Three Pillars (`synthesis/positioning.md`): Innovative Thinking → Technological Application → Endurance.
- Market positioning (`synthesis/positioning.md`): *"a business model architect for organisations with systemic constraints"* … *"Architect — designs, then partners to build."*
- Explicitly NOT (`synthesis/positioning.md`): a traditional management consultancy.

## The site's job

**pecuvate.com presents Pecuvate as a problem-solver that thinks differently and builds what it designs.** Not a consulting firm — Pecuvate implements and builds out the strategies it develops; advisory language alone is banned (see Copy rules). Not the internal OS-of-BFG division — that story appears as proof of skin in the game, not as the front door.

Audiences, in order of priority (from positioning.md's Target Relationships + Year 1 goals):

1. **Organisations with structural constraints** who need their model rebuilt, not advice
2. **Founders / knowledge-holders** seeking a co-creation partner who architects and builds
3. (Future) product customers — not addressed on the site yet

## Hero options

All three revised against the founder directive (implement-and-build, not consult) and sourced from the KB. The choice between them is the open decision this spec presents.

### Option A — Endurance conviction (restore + sharpen)

> **The most enduring businesses aren't built on products. They're built on systems.**
>
> Pecuvate combines innovative thinking with technological application to solve structural problems — designing the business model, then building the system that runs it.

- **Source:** Endurance pillar; promise line (*"To build systems that generate enduring value"*); group mission phrasing.
- **Why it works:** passes the stranger test; conviction-led; it was the original site's strongest line — the 2026-07-21 pass over-corrected by replacing it when only the "venture studio" subtitle was broken.
- **Risk:** doesn't say who it's for until the subtitle; abstract on its own.

### Option B — Think and build (the existing tagline, promoted)

> **Rethinking problems. Building lasting solutions.**
>
> Pecuvate questions how a problem is meant to be solved, then architects the business model and builds the technology that solves it — and keeps it solved.

- **Source:** the approved tagline in `synthesis/positioning.md`, verbatim; problem-first philosophy (*"How could this problem be better solved?"*).
- **Why it works:** already-approved brand language; the think→build pairing is the founder's own framing; shortest and most memorable.
- **Risk:** tagline-as-headline can read generic if the subtitle doesn't carry the specificity — the subtitle is load-bearing here.

### Option C — Problem-first (the rebuild frame)

> **Some problems can't be advised away. They have to be rebuilt.**
>
> Pecuvate diagnoses the structural constraint, redesigns the model, and builds the systems that make the fix permanent — proven on ventures we run ourselves.

- **Source:** *"Most consultants advise. Pecuvate rebuilds business models"* (positioning.md), reworked so "consultant" is never claimed as the category; the Empowr diagnose→design pattern (`entities/empowr-cic.md`).
- **Why it works:** opens on the visitor's situation; implicitly anti-consultancy without anchoring Pecuvate *as* a consultancy.
- **Risk:** negative-first framing; still uses advice as the reference point, which sails close to the category the founder wants to avoid.

## Copy rules (apply to all public Pecuvate surfaces)

1. **Never lead with BFG Alliance.** A stranger can't be introduced to Pecuvate via a bigger unknown. The group appears in the Ecosystem section, introduced on the visitor's terms.
2. **Never define by negation in a hero.** "Not a consultancy. Not a studio." corrects beliefs a first-time visitor doesn't hold. The KB's is/is-not table is internal clarification, not copy.
3. **"Architect" and "build" always travel together.** Advisory-only language ("we advise," "we recommend," "strategy consulting") is banned. Pecuvate designs *and* implements.
4. **No internal jargon unexplained.** "The OS," "delivery modes," "the four functions" — internal vocabulary. If used publicly, it must be earned by the surrounding copy, never assumed.
5. **Disclose the Empowr relationship.** Empowr CIC is a group entity. Presenting it as anonymous client work is a credibility trap for any prospect who looks it up. Disclosed, it becomes the strongest proof available: *we build on the same system we offer.*
6. **No self-assessed £ figures as bare stats.** The £113,800–£188,500 figure is an internal equivalent-market-value estimate about a sister entity. On public surfaces use concrete, verifiable facts instead (six-platform automation, payments, e-signature, payroll, 25-test E2E suite). The full analysis can back a case-study page where its methodology is shown.
7. **The three modes are one method, three vehicles** — never a services list. (This constraint already exists in `Production/01-content/what-pecuvate-does.md`.)
